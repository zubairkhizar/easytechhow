How to Transfer WhatsApp Chats to a New Phone

Short answer: make a fresh WhatsApp backup on your old phone, then install WhatsApp on the new phone, verify the same phone number, and tap Restore when it is offered. The order matters: if you start chatting on the new phone before restoring, the restore option may disappear, and the old chats become much harder to recover. Do the backup first, while both phones are still in your hands.

Before you start: three things to check

First, make sure you know the phone number linked to WhatsApp, and that you can receive a verification code on it — if you have changed numbers, sort that out before the transfer, not during it. Second, check that the new phone is signed into the same cloud account as the old one: a Google account for Android to Android, or the same Apple ID for iPhone to iPhone. Third, connect both phones to Wi-Fi and power, because a large chat history with photos and videos can take a long time to back up and restore, and a phone that runs out of battery halfway through is a recipe for a partial transfer.

1. Make a fresh backup on the old phone

On the old phone, open WhatsApp, go to Settings, then Chats, then Chat backup, and tap Back Up Now. Include videos if you want them transferred, but note that this makes the backup much larger and slower. Wait until the backup finishes and check the time shown: it should be today, not last week. If the backup fails, check your cloud storage space and your internet connection, and free up space if needed. Do not skip this step because an automatic backup ran last night — a fresh backup catches the messages from today as well.

2. Android to Android, or iPhone to iPhone

Install WhatsApp on the new phone and verify the same phone number. When WhatsApp finds a backup in your Google Drive or iCloud account, it will offer to restore it: tap Restore and wait for it to finish before opening any chats. The restore may take a while, and media files continue downloading in the background after the messages appear, so do not panic if photos take a little longer to show up. Keep the phone on Wi-Fi until the media download is complete, and do not uninstall WhatsApp from the old phone until you have checked the new one.

3. Moving between Android and iPhone

Cross-platform transfers are possible, but they need an extra step. When setting up a new iPhone, choose to move data from Android and follow the Move to iOS prompts, which include a WhatsApp option. For Android to iPhone in the other direction, WhatsApp's own transfer during setup, or Samsung Smart Switch on Samsung phones, can move chats directly between the two phones. In both cases, keep both phones nearby, charged and on the same Wi-Fi network, and do not set up WhatsApp on the new phone first, because the transfer option is offered during setup. Our photo transfer guide covers the same move for your gallery, which is a separate step from WhatsApp.

4. If you also changed your number

If your number changed as well as your phone, use the Change Number feature on the old phone first, while the old number is still active: in WhatsApp, go to Settings, then Account, then Change Number, and follow the prompts. Then back up, move to the new phone, and verify the new number. Doing it in this order keeps your chats, groups and contacts linked. If the old number is already inactive and you cannot receive a code on it, you can still start WhatsApp on the new number, but your old chats will only come back through a backup tied to that account, so check the backup before you give up on them.

5. Check the transfer before wiping the old phone

Open several chats on the new phone, including a group, a chat with photos, and a recent conversation, and confirm the messages and media are there. Check that your profile name and photo carried over, and that important starred messages are present. Only when you are satisfied should you remove WhatsApp from the old phone or factory reset it for resale. This check takes two minutes and prevents the most common transfer regret: wiping the only copy of a chat history before realising the restore was incomplete.

Common problems and fixes

If no backup is found, check that the new phone is signed into the same Google or Apple account, and that backup is turned on in WhatsApp on the old phone. If the restore stalls, check your Wi-Fi and storage space on the new phone, then restart the restore rather than starting over with a fresh account. If some media is missing, it may still be downloading: leave the phone on Wi-Fi and check again later. If a chat is missing entirely, it may have been created after your last backup, which is why a fresh backup on the old phone matters so much. And remember that WhatsApp messages are end-to-end encrypted, so no support team can recover a chat that was never backed up.

Keep chats safe after the move

Once the transfer is done, turn on automatic backup in WhatsApp settings and choose a frequency that suits you: daily is best for heavy users, weekly is enough for light use. Also secure the account itself with two-step verification, which our WhatsApp security guide explains step by step, because a transferred account is just as valuable to a hijacker as an old one. Finally, review your linked devices and remove any old phone or computer you no longer use, so your chats stay on devices you actually control.

Quick checklist

Confirm your number and cloud account, make a fresh backup on the old phone, verify the same number on the new phone, tap Restore before chatting, check several chats and media, and only then wipe the old phone. For a platform switch, use the transfer option during setup, not after, and keep both phones charged and on Wi-Fi throughout.

Frequently asked questions

Can I transfer WhatsApp chats without a backup? It is risky. A fresh backup on the old phone is the safest way. Without one, your chats may be lost for good, so back up first while both phones are still in your hands.

What if I changed my phone number? Change the number inside WhatsApp on the old phone first, under Settings, then Account, then Change number. Only then make the backup and move to the new phone.

Will my photos and videos transfer too? Yes, if you include videos in the backup. It makes the backup much larger and slower, so use Wi-Fi and keep both phones plugged in.

Why is there no restore option on the new phone? Usually because you started chatting before restoring, or you signed in with a different number or cloud account. Check the number, the Google account or Apple ID, and that the backup on the old phone is from today.
